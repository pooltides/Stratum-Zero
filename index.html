Institutional mandate

The file is the argument.

Stratum Zero is a clinical vault for the transmission of ancient Near Eastern texts. The unit of work is the file: a verse or pericope set beside the manuscript, the public-domain English of the King James tradition, the behavior of modern translations, and the material receipt. Archaeology, genetics, and comparative linguistics are admitted only when a stratum, a sample, or a catalog number can be named.

Unbound slots are index capacity. They are not findings, and they are not cited. A forensic note is finished when a rival reading has to answer the receipt: the museum number, the siglum, the edition, or the published sample. Harmonies and slogans do not bind a file.

  • Witness Original language of the copy: Hebrew, Aramaic, Greek, or Sanskrit when the file is comparative.
  • 1611 King James tradition in readable 1769 spelling. The translation is public domain.
  • Track How NIV and ESV treat the crux: base text, main reading, footnote. Their full verses are not reprinted.
  • Receipt Excavation, museum serial, critical edition, or genetic sample. No serial is invented to fill a card.
